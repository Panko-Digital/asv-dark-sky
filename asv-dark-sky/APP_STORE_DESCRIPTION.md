## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Measure night sky brightness with your phone camera. ASV Dark Sky converts your smartphone into a Sky Quality Meter (SQM), providing accurate light pollution readings for astronomers and sky enthusiasts.

Developed in collaboration with the Astronomical Society of Victoria, this app uses astrophotography techniques to calculate sky brightness values comparable to commercial SQM devices.

KEY FEATURES

Sky Quality Measurements
Take sky and dark frame photos to measure brightness. The app calculates SQM values (typically 15-22 mag/arcsec²) with automatic moon phase compensation for accuracy throughout the lunar cycle.

Interactive Map
View community measurements on a global map. Color-coded markers indicate sky quality at different locations. Find dark sky sites near you and compare light pollution levels across regions.

Measurement History
All readings are saved with GPS coordinates, timestamps, and moon phase data. Track your favorite observation locations and monitor sky quality changes over time. Export measurement data for further analysis.

Moon Phase Tracking
Real-time moon phase and altitude calculations automatically adjust brightness readings for lunar impact. Visual moon arc shows current position. Get timing recommendations for optimal measurements.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Point your camera at a clear patch of night sky
2. Capture a sky photo
3. Cover your lens and take a dark frame
4. View your instant SQM reading
5. Browse your measurement history and explore the community map

TECHNICAL DETAILS

The app processes images using median sky brightness calculation and professional calibration methods. Dark frame subtraction removes sensor noise for accurate results. Moon brightness compensation accounts for lunar phase and altitude. GPS tagging documents each measurement location.

IDEAL FOR

- Amateur astronomers planning observation sessions
- Documenting local sky conditions
- Finding dark sky locations for astrophotography
- Tracking light pollution patterns
- Learning about sky brightness and light pollution

PRIVACY

Location data is used only for measurement tagging. Measurements are stored securely. You control whether to share data with the community. No personal information is required.
